Privacy Policy - Modded in the Valley

Effective date: November 2, 2025
Owner: Modded in the Valley - Wasilla, Alaska

I operate Modded in the Valley as a sole proprietor based in Wasilla, Alaska.
I provide console modification, repair, and customization services.

This policy explains how I handle information you provide through the Modded in the Valley website, contact forms, direct messaging platforms, email, or by mail when sending in a device.

1. Information I Collect

I only collect information reasonably necessary to provide quotes, communicate with customers, schedule work, and complete services.

Depending on how you contact me or use my website, this may include:

* Your name, display name, or username
* Your email address or other contact information
* Your ZIP code for estimating service availability, shipping, or mail-in options
* Device details, including brand, model, condition, and a description of the issue or requested work
* Photos, files, or notes you voluntarily provide about your device
* Your legal name and shipping or return address if you use mail-in service
* Information submitted through forms on the Modded in the Valley website

I do not intentionally request or store birthdays, payment card information, or other personal information that is not reasonably necessary to provide my services.

2. How I Use the Information

I may use the information you provide:

* To respond to your request or message
* To provide quotes, service information, updates, or instructions
* To schedule and complete modification, repair, or customization services
* To arrange mail-in service, shipping, or device returns
* To maintain basic service, communication, and accounting records
* To follow up regarding work I have completed when necessary
* To operate and maintain the Modded in the Valley website and service process

I do not sell your personal information or use information provided directly to me for unrelated advertising or tracking.

3. Information Sharing

I do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

Information may be shared only when reasonably necessary to:

* Process information through services used to operate the website or contact forms
* Communicate through a third-party platform when you choose to contact me through that platform
* Ship or return devices through USPS or another shipping carrier
* Complete a service you have requested
* Comply with a lawful request or legal obligation

Third-party websites, communication platforms, shipping carriers, and other services may have their own privacy policies and data practices. Information processed by those services may also be subject to their respective policies.

4. Data Retention

I retain service information, such as device details, repair or modification notes, transaction records, and relevant communications, only for as long as reasonably necessary for service records, accounting, legal obligations, or repeat customer reference.

You may request deletion of personal information I maintain about you, subject to information I may need to retain for legitimate business, accounting, legal, or record-keeping purposes.

5. Security

I take reasonable precautions to protect customer information and service records from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or loss.

However, no website, electronic communication system, shipping process, or online storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Third-party services used to communicate with customers or operate portions of the website are subject to their own security and data practices.

6. Children’s Information

Modded in the Valley’s services are intended for adults.

I do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years old. If I become aware that personal information from a child under 13 has been collected, I will take reasonable steps to delete it.

7. Your Rights

You may contact me to:

* Ask what personal information I maintain about you
* Request correction of inaccurate personal information
* Request deletion of your personal information
* Withdraw consent where information is processed based on your consent

Certain information may need to be retained when required for accounting, legal, transaction, fraud prevention, or other legitimate business purposes.

Requests can be submitted through the contact options available on the Modded in the Valley website.
