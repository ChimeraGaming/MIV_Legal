## Accessibility Statement

This website serves as the official online presence for **Modded In The Valley**, a console and handheld modding and repair service. While the content may be hosted through external platforms, it is intended to apply to the Modded In The Valley website accessed through its primary domain.

Modded In The Valley is committed to making this website accessible to as many people as possible. I strive to ensure that the site is usable for all visitors, including those using assistive technologies.

This website is built using the accessibility features provided by the Wix platform. I understand that accessibility needs can vary, and if you experience any difficulty accessing content, navigating the site, or using any features, please reach out so I can assist you.

I welcome feedback on improving accessibility and will make reasonable efforts to address concerns when possible.

**Contact:**  
You can reach me through the website’s messaging system or via the contact methods listed on this site.
